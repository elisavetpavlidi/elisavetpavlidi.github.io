01 / A little about me

About me

Ελισάβετ Παυλίδη

I'm a physics undergraduate specializing in astrophysics at the University of Athens. My interests lie in early universe cosmology, general relativity, black hole physics and gravitational waves.

I enjoy connecting theoretical models with numerical methods and interactive scientific visualizations: turning equations into something we can explore.

Team Greece · Oklahoma State University, USAInternational Physicists’ Tournament 2026+

I represented Greece at the International Physicists’ Tournament 2026 at Oklahoma State University, bringing months of preparation to an international arena of experimental physics and scientific debate.

As a member of Team Greece, I worked at the intersection of theory, experiment and computation: developing physical explanations, testing them against measurements, and defending our conclusions in discussion with teams from around the world.

My work on Crazy Cylinder combined hydrodynamic modelling, experimental investigation and numerical analysis to understand the oscillations of a cylinder moving through confined fluid. The presentation is publicly archived on Zenodo.

Team Greece ↗My presentation ↗
Selected team member · Second year undergraduate · Warsaw, PolandInternational Physicists’ Tournament 2025+

As a second year undergraduate, I was selected for the final team representing Greece and the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens at IPT 2025 in Warsaw, under the supervision of Prof. Theocharis Apostolatos. I earned my place through a competitive selection process, ahead of candidates in their fourth and fifth years of study.

At the IPT Conference, Maria Giga and I presented our work on PET Bottle Rocket, sharing our investigation with an international audience. This experience connected the sustained work of preparing a physics problem with the challenge of communicating and discussing it on an international stage.

The presentation is publicly archived on Zenodo.
